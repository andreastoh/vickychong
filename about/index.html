The Early Narrative

Raised in an extended family of readers, Vicky’s journey began in the quiet corners of second-hand bookshops. Her fascination with the human condition led her to pursue a career that bridges the gap between traditional journalism and contemporary fiction.

Her work often explores the intersection of cultural heritage and modern identity, drawing inspiration from her extensive travels across the globe and her deep-seated love for the human connection.

Craft & Inspiration

She draws inspiration from the works of the late Irish writer Maeve Binchy and contemporary masters of the short form like Alice Munro.
